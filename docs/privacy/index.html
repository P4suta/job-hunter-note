Job Hunter Note Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Job Hunter Note (未来探しくん) stores employer names, application stages, deadlines, and submission history in the app’s storage on your device.

The developer collects no app data and uses no account system, advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device.

You control where notifications and their previews appear in Settings.

Retention and removal

Deleting the app removes its journal from the device.

Device backups may include app data according to your backup settings.

You can manage existing backups in iCloud or on the computer that holds them.

Support requests

When you open a support link, your browser connects to the website you select.

GitHub publishes the information you choose to post in an issue.

For questions about the app’s data practices, visit support.
